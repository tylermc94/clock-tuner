What beat rate should I use?

The beat rate (beats-per-hour, BPH) is a property of the movement, not something you pick — it's what the clock is designed to run at. A 1-second "seconds pendulum" is 3600 BPH (one tick every second). Check the movement's paperwork or stamp. Don't know it? Use one of the two tools below while the clock is running.

🎯 Calibrate from observed drift — most accurate

The mic only measures the beat interval — it can't know the intended rate on its own. If you've compared the clock to a reference over time, tell me how far it drifted and I'll compute the exact beat rate, so the reading and the pendulum advice match reality.
